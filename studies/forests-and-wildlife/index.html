Getting startedWhat is a Forest?

Forests are large areas of land covered mainly with trees and other small plants. The kind of trees and plants that grow in a forest depend on the climate and soil of the place.

🌿 Natural Vegetation

Forests grow naturally in a region — that's why they are called natural vegetation.

🏡 Natural Habitat

Forests provide shelter to many wild animals — that's why they are called the natural habitat of the animals.

India has a great diversity of natural ecosystems, and our forests can be divided into five main groups. Explore each one below!

DiscoverRole & Uses of Forests

Like soil and minerals, forests are also an important natural resource. They provide natural habitat — food, water and shelter — to a large number of living things.

🏢 Tap a layer of the forest

Every layer of the forest is home to different animals.

🍽️ Every Part of a Tree is Food for Someone

Birds eat fruits and seeds, deer eat leaves, squirrels eat nuts, some insects eat roots, some drink sap, and many animals eat green leaves. Some forest animals also feed upon other smaller and weaker animals.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Homes for Tribal Communities

Many tribal communities have been living in forests since ancient times. They depend on the forests for food, shelter and medicines.

The Chipko Movement

The word 'Chipko', literally in Hindi, means "to hug." It was a movement of the peasants in the Uttarakhand region of India to prevent the felling of trees by wood contractors. It was strengthened by Sunder Lal Bahuguna, and the movement spread to other parts of the Himalayas.

🌳

Van Mahotsav — Conservation of Our Forest Wealth

A festival called Van Mahotsav is celebrated every year throughout India in the first week of July to encourage people to plant trees and get involved with conservation and protection of nature. Each one of us must take this opportunity to restore the greenery in our surroundings, instead of cutting it down.
