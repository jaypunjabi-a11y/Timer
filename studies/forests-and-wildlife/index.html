Getting startedForest Intro and Types

🌱 What is a Forest?

Forests are large areas of land covered mainly with trees and other small plants. The kind of trees and plants that grow in a forest depend on the climate and soil of the place.

🌿 Natural Vegetation

Forests grow naturally in a region — that's why they are called natural vegetation.

🏡 Natural Habitat

Forests provide shelter to many wild animals — that's why they are called the natural habitat of the animals.

India has a great diversity of natural ecosystems, and our forests can be divided into five main groups. Explore each one alongside!

DiscoverRole & Uses of Forests

Like soil and minerals, forests are also an important natural resource. They provide natural habitat — food, water and shelter — to a large number of living things.

🏢 Tap a layer of the forest

Every layer of the forest is home to different animals.

🍽️ Every Part of a Tree is Food for Someone

Birds eat fruits and seeds, deer eat leaves, squirrels eat nuts, some insects eat roots, some drink sap, and many animals eat green leaves. Some forest animals also feed upon other smaller and weaker animals.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Homes for Tribal Communities

Many tribal communities have been living in forests since ancient times. They depend on the forests for food, shelter and medicines.

The Chipko Movement

The word 'Chipko', literally in Hindi, means "to hug." It was a movement of the peasants in the Uttarakhand region of India to prevent the felling of trees by wood contractors. It was strengthened by Sunder Lal Bahuguna, and the movement spread to other parts of the Himalayas.

🌳

Van Mahotsav — Conservation of Our Forest Wealth

A festival called Van Mahotsav is celebrated every year throughout India in the first week of July to encourage people to plant trees and get involved with conservation and protection of nature. Each one of us must take this opportunity to restore the greenery in our surroundings, instead of cutting it down.

DiscoverNational Parks & Wildlife Sanctuaries

🏞️ National Park

A protected area to conserve the natural environment and the wildlife in it, and also provide recreation (an area of environmental importance or natural beauty protected by the state which the public may visit). Killing, hunting, shooting or capturing of any species of bird or animal is not allowed.

🦌 Wildlife Sanctuary

A protected area, usually in natural condition, through the management of human activities to provide and maintain the habitat of wild animals and the habitats that support them. Killing, hunting, shooting or capturing of any species of bird or animal is not allowed.

Toxin, Poison or Venom?

A toxin is any substance that can cause harm.
A poison is a toxin that causes its effect through ingestion or absorption — it could be anything in excess, be it food, water, etc.
A venom is a toxin that causes its effect through contact with the blood stream. Its administration is via a specialized introductory/delivery system — it is a liquid substance that needs to be injected or get in touch with the bloodstream in order to cause serious discomfort, and can eventually be fatal (lead to death).

Wrap upChapter Recap

🌟 Let's Recall

  • Forests are large areas of land covered mainly with trees and other small plants. They are called the natural habitat of the animals.
  • Our forests can be divided into five main groups — Evergreen forests, Deciduous forests, Thorn forests, Mountain or Coniferous forests, and Marshy or tidal forests.
  • The wildlife found in the countries forming the Sub-continent (namely India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, and the island countries of Sri Lanka and Maldives) are very similar.
  • Loss of habitat is the prime reason for the decrease in the number of wild animals. Hence, the government has declared several areas as protected areas — National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries.

🌱 Now You Know...

  • Earth's natural resources are very important for the survival of all living beings. Forests are a renewable but exhaustible natural resource.
  • If forests are used up and not replaced for a long period of time, the forest cover will disappear.
  • Forests are one of the most valuable natural resources, which have taken millions of years to grow. But these resources are being used faster than they can be replenished by nature.
  • Man should make judicious (wise) use of natural resources.
